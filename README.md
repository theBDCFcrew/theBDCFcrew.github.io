# 🌴 Los Santos Weekly Hub

> **Live Web App**: [https://thebdcfcrew.github.io](https://thebdcfcrew.github.io)

A streamlined, modern web dashboard for tracking **GTA Online Weekly Updates**, featuring live reset countdowns, interactive daily and weekly checklists, multiplier breakdowns, searchable discounts, and automated 1-click Google Sheets synchronization.

---

## ✨ Features

- ⏳ **Live Weekly Reset Countdown**: Automatically counts down to the weekly Rockstar reset every Thursday at 5:00 AM ET / 10:00 AM UTC.
- 🎯 **Weekly Tasks & Rides**: Track Casino Podium spins, LS Car Meet Prize Ride challenges, Weekly Challenges, Salvage Yard Robberies, and Time Trials.
- 📅 **Daily Objectives Streak Tracker**: 7-day interactive checklist (Thursday – Wednesday) with streak reward metrics and local progress saving.
- 💰 **Dynamic Multipliers & Bonuses**: Real-time detection and display of all active payout multipliers (5X, 4X, 3X, 2.5X, 2X, 1.5X, and production speed boosts) with filter pills.
- 🏷️ **Strict Discounts & Deals Search**: Filter vehicles, properties, weapons, apparel, and freebies without clutter.
- 🏬 **Showrooms & Gun Van**: View Luxury Autos, Simeon's Premium Deluxe Motorsport stock, Gun Van inventory, and GTA+ perks.
- 📊 **Google Sheets 1-Click Sync**: Export formatted data to your Google Sheet's `Weekly Update` tab via clipboard TSV or automated Google Apps Script (`GtaWeeklyUpdate.gs`).
